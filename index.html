About Us

About Us photo

PAH/NA

PAH/NA is a rebrand of a small startup business based in the Philippines. It is inspired by the Tagalog word “Pahina,” which means a single leaf of a book.

The slash symbolizes how each page of a book is a different experience, representing our collective experiences and stories we want to share. Our business aims to bring comfort and freedom of expression to our customers through our shirts and other products.

We aim to give Filipinas
a way to express themselves by making everyday
clothing feel a lot more personal.

PAH/NA

Every page holds a story. PAH/NA is here to celebrate your voice, empower women, and keep your story going through every season of life.
